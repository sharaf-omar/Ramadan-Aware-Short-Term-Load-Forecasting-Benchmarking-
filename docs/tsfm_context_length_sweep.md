# TSFM Context-Length Sensitivity Sweep (Ablation C)

Tests the proposal hypothesis that longer contexts spanning Ramadan transitions
improve regime-shift forecast accuracy.

## Setup

- 4 TSFMs × 4 context lengths (L ∈ {96, 168, 336, 720}) = 16 inference passes.
- Univariate framing throughout (Hijri covariates are Ablation A, see
  `docs/tsfm_hijri_covariates.md`).
- Single seed = 0.
- Test window 2024-01-01 .. 2025-03-31 (10,944 hours).

## Aggregate MAE (intersection-τ across all L per model)

| Model              | L=96   | L=168  | L=336  | L=720  | Best |
|--------------------|--------|--------|--------|--------|------|
| Chronos-Bolt-Base  | 1831.3 | 1093.7 | 1008.0 |  968.9 | **L=720** |
| TimesFM 2.5-200M   | 1666.9 | 1173.2 | 1375.8 | 1334.5 | **L=168** |
| Moirai-1.1-R-Small | 2428.4 | 1873.8 | 1727.1 | 1771.2 | **L=336** |
| Time-MoE-200M      | 1893.1 | 1170.4 | 1034.6 |  985.9 | **L=720** |

**Headline:**
- **Chronos and Time-MoE improve monotonically with L** — longer context always
  helps. Both reach their best at L=720 hours (~30 days), which spans a full
  Ramadan transition.
- **TimesFM peaks at L=168** (1 week). Non-monotone: longer contexts hurt.
  This is unusual and may reflect TimesFM's patch-size assumption optimizing
  for week-length context windows during pretraining.
- **Moirai peaks at L=336** (2 weeks). Mild U-shape.
- **L=96 is uniformly bad** for all 4 TSFMs. 4 days of context is not enough
  for hourly STLF — confirms the proposal's expectation that short contexts
  miss the weekly seasonality.

## Findings

1. **Proposal hypothesis confirmed (partially):** longer contexts that span
   Ramadan transitions do improve regime-shift forecasting — but only for the
   two univariate models (Chronos, Time-MoE). On those, **Heatwave MAE drops
   45%** from L=96 → L=720 on Chronos (2160 → 1221), and **39%** on Time-MoE
   (2235 → 1268). Ramadan MAE drops ~38% on both.

2. **TimesFM and Moirai have non-monotone L curves.** TimesFM peaks at L=168;
   Moirai at L=336. The architectures interact with context length differently
   than pure decoder-only models. This is a separate publishable observation:
   "context-length sensitivity is architecture-specific; no universal optimum."

3. **L=720 winners (Chronos, Time-MoE) approach the LightGBM baseline.**
   - LightGBM-hijri (regime Normal): **874**
   - Chronos L=720 (regime Normal): **904** (+3.4%)
   - Time-MoE L=720 (regime Normal): **909** (+4.0%)

   On Heatwave, both TSFMs *beat* LightGBM (1693) at L=720:
   - Chronos L=720: **1221** (–28%)
   - Time-MoE L=720: **1268** (–25%)

4. **Ramadan is where TSFMs lose to LGBM.** LGBM-hijri Ramadan = 800 MW; the
   best TSFM (Chronos L=720) = 1061 MW (+33%). Ablation A (Hijri covariates
   on TimesFM and Moirai) tests whether covariate-capable TSFMs can close
   this gap.
